# Bundled fonts

## Anton (`anton-latin-400.woff2`)
- **Designer:** Vernon Adams (maintained by the Google Fonts team).
- **License:** SIL Open Font License 1.1 — <https://openfontlicense.org/>.
- **Source:** <https://github.com/googlefonts/anton> (via the Fontsource mirror
  `cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/anton@latest/latin-400-normal.woff2`).
- **Subset:** Latin only (~19 KB).
- **Use:** the "display" face for host-TV headlines / big moments
  (`--font-display`). One weight, all-caps by design.

OFL summary: free to use, bundle and embed, including in commercial work; the
font itself may not be sold on its own and derivatives may not use the reserved
name "Anton". We only bundle the unmodified `.woff2`.
